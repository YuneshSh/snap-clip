How it works

Auto-capture Detected on every page copy event and on popup open for OS screenshots.
One-click re-copy Hover any card and click Copy to put it back on your clipboard instantly.
Download any shot Save screenshots as PNG files directly from the popup.
Configurable limit 1 – 50 slots. Oldest auto-removed when full (circular buffer).
Local storage only Screenshots stay in your browser. Nothing is sent to any server.
