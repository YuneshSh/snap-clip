# SnapClip Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 17, 2026

SnapClip (“we”, “the extension”) is a Chrome extension that saves screenshots, text, and links from your clipboard so you can reuse them later.

## Data we store on your device

SnapClip stores the following **locally** in Chrome extension storage on your computer:

- Screenshots / images you capture or paste  
- Text clips and links you save  
- Quick notes and calendar date notes you create  
- Settings (theme, save limits, UI preferences)  
- Pro license key and activation status (if you purchase Pro)

This data stays on your device unless you choose to copy or export it. We do not operate a SnapClip account system that uploads your clips to our servers.

## Optional native companion (Pro)

If you install the optional native companion for system-wide capture, it runs on your computer and may read clipboard changes to forward new items into SnapClip. That data is processed locally and is not uploaded to SnapClip servers by the companion.

## Purchases (SnapClip Pro)

Pro purchases are processed by **Lemon Squeezy** (merchant of record). When you buy Pro, Lemon Squeezy may collect payment details, email, and billing information under their own privacy policy. SnapClip’s license API may receive order-related metadata needed to issue and validate a license key (for example order identifiers and email associated with the purchase). We use that only to fulfill and validate your Pro license.

License checkout and validation requests go to our license service hosted on Cloudflare Workers.

## Permissions

SnapClip requests Chrome permissions only to provide its features (clipboard read/write, local storage, optional native messaging, alarms for connection/license maintenance, and limited page access to support in-browser capture). We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

## Data sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal data. Limited data may be processed by:

- **Google Chrome / your device** — local storage  
- **Lemon Squeezy** — payment and checkout  
- **Cloudflare** — hosting the license API  

## Your choices

You can clear saved clips from SnapClip settings, remove the extension (which removes local extension data), or uninstall the native companion. For purchase/billing questions, contact Lemon Squeezy support or the email below.
